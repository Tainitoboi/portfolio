DARKST4R
2025
DARKST4R is a 2D animated video that concerns the story of a little darkstar, delving into its life, from the moment of its birth and continuing with its growth and formation. All the animations were first drawn digitally in Photoshop, and then put together in After Effects, where the entire video was edited.
Created by Tainito.
