Carlo Marteo
2024
Carlo Marteo is a custom Funko Pop figure that I made as a Christmas gift for a friend of mine. Starting from the original design, the figure takes up of the distinctive features of the person, especially in hair, clothes and accessories. The packaging is also customized, comprising inside jokes that replace the original elements, such as texts and logos. The figure has been 3D printed in a smaller monochromatic version.
Created by Tainito.
