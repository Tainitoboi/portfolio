La Settimana Enigmistica - ASCII
2024
The special issue of the famous italian weekly “La settimana enigmistica” entirely dedicated to ASCII art was created with the aim of transposing on a digital art current on a paper artifact. The graphic visuals are entirely made in ASCII art and also the games and information inside the magazine deal with topics related to this artistic current.
Created with: A. Pedrini, M. Semeraro, B. Tronconi, C. Turi, I. Urgesi.
