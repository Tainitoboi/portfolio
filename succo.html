SUCCO
2023
Succo is a website created from the desire to discover and shed light on the daily problems affecting the various campuses of the Politecnico di Milano. With a colorful and playful mood, the homonymous mascot gives the students more information and solutions about these issues. Through the Instagram stories students can monitor what’s happening in the campus' spaces and report the problems they find putting stickers around the university. Visit the website and the ig profile.
Created with: E. Bailetti, G. Basile, F. Iannicelli, F. Signorile, D. Vitrano.
