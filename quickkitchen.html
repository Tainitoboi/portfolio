Quick Kitchen
2023
Quick Kitchen is my first animated short made with two of my friends, recreating a scene from the movie "X-Men: Days of Future Past", in which Quicksilver tries to help Magneto escape from maximum security prison. Compared to the original, it adopts a cartoonish style, which often takes up the universe of American comics, especially in the character design. All the scenes are modeled and animated in Blender and the post-production is made in After Effects.
Created with: A. Ruggiu and D. Vitrano.
