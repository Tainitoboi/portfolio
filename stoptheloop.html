Stop The Loop
2025
Stop The Loop is a proposal campaign designed by Syncro that aims at raising awareness regarding each one's pace of life. The campaign targets Generation Z, always immersed in a hyper-connected world and subject to constant social pressures that often make it difficult to slow down and maintain the focus on personal well-being.

Syncro understands rhythm as an interpretive lens for observing and understanding reality. Stop The Loop therefore aims to offer concrete tools to help young people reflect, find their own rhythm and adopt conscious choices, promoting an authentic culture of personal well-being.
Discover more about the project on the website.
Created with: A. Battaglia, A. Brolatti, A. Cela, A. Cupisti, A. De Nicolo.
