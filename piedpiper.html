The Pied Piper
2022
The Pied Piper is a 2D platform game based on the homonymous fairy tale by the Brothers Grimm created with three of my university mates. Starting from the original story, the plot introduces a small twist: the piper has forgotten his magic melody and then must leave in search of the missing notes throughout the city infested by rats.
All visuals and animations are created with self made sprites and assets, using the pixel art style to remind the retro arcade games. The map develops on three areas (the lodge, the sewers and the cathedral) with different looks and enemy difficulty. Interacting with other characters and completing the mission, the piper can also collect the rat’s teeth and upgrade his pipe to unlock new powers.
Created with: M. Dell’Agostino, A, Marenghi, D. Vitrano.
